# Part 5 – Final Reflection
Write **200–300 words** describing your learning experience during this lab.
Consider:
* What surprised you about computer hardware?
* What components were new to you?
* What challenges did you face?
* What did you learn about embedded systems?

During this lab I learned how the physical parts of a computer work together to run software. What surprised me most was how dependent every component is on the others; the CPU cannot do useful work without RAM to hold data, storage to load programs, and a power supply to deliver stable voltages. Seeing the inside of the computer made the abstract idea of "processing" feel physical and real.

Several components were new to me, especially the motherboard's role as the central communication hub and the way the Raspberry Pi combines so many functions into a single System on a Chip. I also found the GPIO pins interesting because they allow the Pi to control real-world electronics, which is quite different from a normal desktop PC.

The main challenge was identifying components confidently and understanding their specifications, since many parts look similar and are covered by heatsinks or cables. Handling the hardware safely and avoiding static damage also required care.

From embedded systems such as the Raspberry Pi I learned that integration is the key design goal. By placing the CPU, memory, networking, and I/O on one small board, engineers achieve low cost, low power use, and high reliability. This lab gave me a much clearer appreciation of how hardware and software depend on each other to create a working computer.
