# Part 2 – Computer Disassembly
## Before Opening the Computer
Describe the computer before opening it.

The system is a compact desktop/tower unit with a metal and plastic chassis. On the outside it has a power button, USB ports, audio jacks, and ventilation grilles. The case is held together by screws at the rear and side panel. Before opening, the machine is powered off completely and the power cable is unplugged so that no live voltage remains. The exterior appears clean with minor dust on the vents, and the side panel is removable to expose the internal components.

## Internal Layout
Open the computer case and observe the internal components.

With the side panel removed, the motherboard is visible mounted to the base of the case. The CPU sits under a heatsink and fan near the centre, with RAM sticks slotted to its right. A power supply unit occupies the top rear corner, and storage drives are mounted in a drive bay at the front. Flat ribbon-like cables and coloured power cables route between the motherboard, drives, and PSU. Cooling is provided by the CPU fan and at least one case fan. Dust is visible on fan blades and heatsink fins, showing the need for periodic cleaning.
